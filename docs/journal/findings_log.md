Measured facts (2026-10-18) for the Cambridge Battlecode bot in `bots/main`:
- Camps never regenerate health, never chase, whole group wakes when one is hit, each camp hits its nearest champion. Standing on spawn heals ~fully in one round.
- Every SDK accessor is a host call (~40 fuel): `bitboard::is_set`, `distance_field::get`, `camp.health()` etc. Read raw structs via `cambc::detail::sdk_champion` / `sdk_camp` once per unit per turn. iostream printing is expensive too.
- Always test with `--release` (platform runs release); tools/batch.py does this.
- Platform replays (`cambc matches download`) include our team stdout; `cambc matches summary --json` gives per-champion metrics and events (deaths with killer).
- Top teams draft Cassi&Issac, Stellar, Ren/Varg/Ryth; buy Monster emblem then Power shards of main stat (+Durability emblem of Health in PvP maps); concentrate gold on Varg/Ren/Stellar. On rows they clear each wolf triple every ~53 rounds and finish ~354 rounds.
- Our biggest losses vs mid/top teams come from PvP deaths (enemy Stellar bursts ~3.4x focus), not farming speed.
- Unranked quota: 30 match-groups per window; our team id 118dbffc-87ec-48a6-ac9c-dc1e371ba364; `tools/group.py <groupId>` summarises results. Test panel: teams 5095aa78 (rank ~60) and 3ad833e0 (rank ~100) on maps swirl,fragment,corners,packs,elkring.

- Progress log (2026-10-18): c9 (platform v11) went 5-5 on the panel vs earlier 1-9/2-8. Biggest single wins: fixing fuel caps, banking at >=30 gems, kiting out of spell range, softening the enemy-sighting penalty (x1.5 -> x0.5) so we farm contested center gem clusters. `tools/farm.py BOT 400` = gems by round 400 vs starter (c9 ~9.7k).

- Engine is deterministic: same two bots + map = identical result, so the 15-game panel (`bash tools/panel.sh BOT` then `python -I tools/panel_result.py IDS`) is an exact A/B test. Unranked quota is ~30 matches/hour (≈6 groups), so retry group creation in a background loop.
