# Balance patch, 2026-10-20

Live on the ladder around 03:00 UTC on October 20 (match `engineVersion` "1.0.0"; local CLI **cambc 3.0.1**). Source:
the patch notes the user relayed, checked against the 3.0.1 SDK and a settings probe (`bots/SetProbe`).

|---|---|
| Cassi & Issac | Issac strength scaling 4.75 → 3, HP scaling 95 → 80; Cassi HP 700 → 600, strength scaling 3.5 → 3, focus scaling 15 → 13, HP scaling 71.25 → 65 |

| Varg | strength 50 → 40 |

| Tavi (rework) | stun damage 2.2 × focus → 1.4 × focus; strength 35 → 45 |

**New champion: Heart of Ruin** (melee; draft index 11, `champion_type` 12). 1000 HP (+100 per shard), 40 strength
(+5), 100 focus (+15). Passive: up to +50% damage from every source, scaling with missing health (25% at half
health). S1: shield self for 1.5 × focus for 5 turns. S2: a 2-tile dash that, on landing, deals 1.75 × focus and stuns
enemies and camps within Chebyshev 1 for 1 turn.

### Follow-up nerf (reported by Eric, October 20 ~21:00 UTC)

Heart of Ruin's growth per matching shard: **Strength 5 → 4, Focus 15 → 12** (health unchanged). The local cambc
3.0.1 had no update at the time, so local games still used the old values; the platform's games use the new ones. The
bot reads live stats, so no code depends on the numbers. Focus is still Heart of Ruin's best stat per shard (12 Focus
= 21 more dash damage, against 4 Strength).

### Second balance patch (announced by Eric ~02:55 UTC, October 21; "the LAST changes until after the sprint")

**Not live on the ladder as of 04:10 UTC, October 21.** The `engineVersion` "1.0.1" that ladder matches report is
the October 20 evening build (the Heart of Ruin nerf): v198's games at 02:02 already carried it, and v200's games at
03:00 and at 04:00 gave identical results (Nyx −6,586 in both, the same as v197's at 01:34), which the new champion
numbers would have changed. Watch for a new `engineVersion` (or changed outcomes for the same two submissions) to know
when it lands; matches are played within seconds of a group's creation and are fully deterministic for the same two
submissions and map.

| Champion | Change |
|---|---|
| Stellar (nerf) | HP 750 → **650**, HP per shard 75 → 65; Focus 85 → **70**, Focus per shard 9 → 7 |
| Heart of Ruin (nerf) | Strength 40 → **30**, Strength per shard 4 → 3.5; dash landing hit 1.75 × Focus → **1.5 × Focus** |
| Ren (nerf) | stun damage 1.25 × Focus → **0.75 × Focus** |
| Vesper Vale (buff) | HP 900 → **1000**, Strength 50 → 55 |
| Vendetta (buff) | HP 800 → **1000** |
| Tavi (buff) | HP 750 → **900**, Focus 100 → 110 |
| Prism (buff) | passive Focus per carried gem 1 → **1.5** |

The devs also announced a hotfix deploy (slow queues during the compile-cache invalidation). The bot reads live stats,
so no code depends on the numbers; what changes is the drafting value of each champion (Stellar is now the squishiest
caster at 650 HP; Prism with a 40-gem bag has 140 Focus, more than Stellar's 70) and the Heart of Ruin dash (150 at
100 Focus instead of 175). The local cambc 3.0.1 engine plays the October 20 numbers; **cambc 3.0.4** (PyPI
win_amd64 wheel uploaded 02:18 UTC, installed as `%LOCALAPPDATA%/cambc/bin/cambc-3.0.4.exe`) plays the new ones
(checked with a base-stats dump: Stellar 650 HP / 70 Focus, Tavi 900 HP / 110 Focus), so local screens use it from
03:40 UTC on.

## Systems

- Kill reward 50 → **100 gold**.
- Wards: cooldown 1 → **10 rounds** (a ward can be placed or moved only every 10 rounds; wards stay permanent).
- Altar lock HP 3 → 5.

## SDK and platform

- New enum values for the camp (`camp_type::golem`) and champion (`heart_of_ruin` in `champion_draft` and
  `champion_type`), and a new passive (`missing_health_damage`).
- `ability_dash` now carries `range`, `radius`, `modifier` and `duration` (Heart of Ruin's landing hit).
- Old submissions that relied on the old patterns no longer compile. **Their teams are out of matchmaking until they
  resubmit** (dev announcement). This is why Intercontinental Fish showed "no eligible bot" on October 20.
- Refresh a workspace with `cambc init --yes .` (SDK) and `cambc maps pull --force` (maps).

## What it did to the game (measured)

- Games last **1,300–1,600 rounds** (previously 400–700) and still usually end on 1000 gems.
- Gems moved into **monoliths** wherever a map has them (see `camps-and-maps.md`); elsewhere elk camps are the main
  gem source and wolves are mostly gold.
- Gold matters far more: top teams earned about twice our gold in the first post-patch games (camp gold 8.8k vs 4.7k a
  game). Valuing gold higher in camp choice was our first post-patch gain (v188).
