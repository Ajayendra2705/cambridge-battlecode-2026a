3. FIX ONE MISTAKE AT A TIME, biggest first:
   - Find the exact cause. If a rule is unclear, probe it (as bots/mt1 and bots/mt2 do) and add
     the result to MECHANICS.md.
   - Build the fix on top of the best bot. Diff it against the base, debug-print to prove the new
     logic fires, then remove the debug. Build-check it.
   - Local check: the 560-game run (pool above, all maps, --jobs 6). Include the base, or compare
     with hb4 at 384/560. It must not be worse. Differences under about 10 wins are noise.
   - Platform check: the full panel at GATE_OFFSET=20, compared game by game with rcmp.py.
     - Noise is about ±5 wins per 100 games.
     - Lost-games-only replays give false positives (selection bias).
     - A fix needs a clear gain, ideally confirmed by a second panel at another offset.
   - Keep a fix only if it clearly helps on the platform. Otherwise log why and try the next idea.
4. REPEAT

HOW TO JUDGE A FIX (opponents update their bots, so win counts drift over time)
- Analysis comes first. The goal is to find and remove our mistakes, not to chase win counts.
- Before building a fix, name its target metric: the exact number the mistake shows up in.
  Examples:
  - elk_arena: deaths before round 300; deaths within 10 tiles of our spawn.
  - Flee fix: deaths while fleeing; damage taken per flee round.
  - Banking: gems lost per death; tributes by round 600.
- Measure that metric in both the base and the variant replays (logging copies, V/D/A lines,
  analysis scripts) and write both numbers to cambc-status.md.
- Read the result like this:
  - The metric did not move: the fix failed, whatever the wins say. Find out why from the replays.
  - The metric moved, but wins are flat: the fix works and another mistake is now the bottleneck.
    Keep it as a stacking candidate if it is not worse.
  - The metric moved and wins are up: keep it.
- Same-time comparison: run base and variant back to back as base -> variant -> base on the same
  games (same GATE_OFFSET).
  - If the two base runs disagree by much more than the noise, the opponents changed mid-test.
    Discard that comparison and redo it.
  - Never compare a variant with a baseline played many hours earlier.
- Old results to recheck with this method: th2, fp1 and bt8l were judged only by win counts
  against an old baseline. Their logged replays (rk_replays/) can still be checked against their
  target metrics.
